Train AI practitioners, not only experts.

The AI Developer diploma, led by Microsoft × Simplon since 2018, has proven that operational profiles can be trained at scale to integrate AI in SMBs and local authorities.

The problem

France trains at the extremes: more than 70% of AI graduates hold a Master's degree or higher (LinkedIn source). But generative AI cannot scale with PhDs alone: it needs practitioners who can integrate it into the daily operations of SMBs and local authorities. This intermediate level is what is missing.

Our conclusion and the policy challenge

The diploma works. The challenge now is to reach the scale of tens of thousands of graduates. Schools alone will not be enough. Public policy must take ownership. This co-funding model is the only way to turn this craft-scale model into a mass response.

Program details

Program
Microsoft × Simplon AI Schools — launched in 2018 on the Issy-les-Moulineaux campus and expanded to most French regions.
Dual impact
For companies: train talent able to deploy AI solutions in production. For workers: provide a qualifying reskilling pathway when jobs change or disappear.
Diploma
RNCP level 6 "AI Developer" (Bachelor-equivalent), 19 months including apprenticeship, plus certifications.
Model
Public-private co-funding (companies + Regions + France Travail). Without this co-funding, no large-scale rollout.
Extensions
GenIAles (7,000 women trained in generative AI fundamentals), IAGenGo (non-profit).
